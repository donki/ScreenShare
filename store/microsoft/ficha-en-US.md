## Description

```
sOC ScreenShare records one screen, all of them or a window, with its sound, to an MP4 ready to share (H.264 + AAC, lightweight by default), in a couple of clicks or with a keyboard shortcut from anywhere. It can also start recording by itself when a given app's window opens.

WHAT TO RECORD
• One screen (picked on a drawing of your displays), all of them together or a single window.

SOUND
• Everything playing through an output device, or just one app.
• When recording a window, only its own sound: anything else playing on the PC stays out.
• Optional microphone, with per-source volume and level meters.

QUALITY
• "Best for sharing" (up to 1080p, about 30 MB per minute) or "High" (up to 4K and 60 fps); or custom: resolution, frame rate, bitrate, H.264 or H.265/HEVC and audio.

AUTOMATIC RECORDING
• Rules per app (and per window title, if you like): when the window opens it's recorded with its sound, and saved when it closes.

CONTROLS
• Record, pause and stop with buttons or global shortcuts (Ctrl+Shift+F9 / F10), also from the icon next to the clock.
• When done: play, show in folder or copy the file to paste into a chat or email.

No internet connection, nothing uploaded. No account, no ads, no trackers. Free software under the MIT licence. In English and Spanish, light and dark mode.
```
